# 20 Below Character Sheet

An [Owlbear Rodeo](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/) extension: your [20 Below](https://20belowrpg.com/) character at the table. Load the character you exported from the Character Creator and everything on the sheet is here, working.

- **Vitals:** the five Elements and their sub-stats, Defense, Social and Mental Defense, Movement (click it for Dash, Sprint, jumps and travel) and Carry. Health, Poise and Sanity with − and +, showing Unconscious, Dying, Flustered and the rest as they happen; Poise and Sanity reset at their floors the way the rules say. Ki, Fate Tokens with the per-Scene spend limit, Exhausted with its effects, Short rest, Full night's rest and New Scene.
- **Skills:** every Skill with its Element and Tier, Boons, Flaws and Resources.
- **Gifts:** each Gift at its Level, with its Ki cost, Adders and Limiters. Click one for everything it does for your character.
- **Gear:** weapons (including Gift attacks), armour that takes and repairs damage, and equipment.
- **Notes:** Nature, Descriptors, scars, experience, Backstory and Notes.
- **Roll dice:** Initiative, an Attack read Normal, at Advantage and at Disadvantage with the damage it deals, a Skill roll, and a Gift Check. Every roll goes to the room's log, so the whole table sees it.

**Save** downloads the character with its tracked numbers, to open in the Creator next session.

## Installing

In Owlbear Rodeo, open the extensions menu and add a custom extension using this URL:

```
https://feralucce.github.io/20_Below_Dice/manifest.json
```

A room that installed it when it was 20 Below Dice already has it; the address has not changed.

## Where it comes from

Everything here except this README and `icon.svg` is generated by `scripts/sync-playsheet.mjs` in the main [20_Below](https://github.com/feralucce/20_Below) repo, from `playsheet/` and the Character Creator's own modules - edit it there, not here. `rules-data.json` is the rules, parsed by the Creator's own parsers.

Outside Owlbear Rodeo (opening `index.html` directly) it works the same, but rolls stay on your page.
